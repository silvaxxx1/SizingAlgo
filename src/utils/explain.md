# V2G Microgrid Optimization Visualization Interpretation 📊

This document provides an interpretation of the plots generated by the `utils/visualization.py` script for a Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) microgrid optimization model. The analysis is based on simulated data and covers optimization convergence, energy flows, Monte Carlo results, economic metrics, component sizing, and parameter sensitivity.

---

## 📈 Plot Interpretations

### 1. Optimization Convergence
- **Source**: `plot_convergence` with `convergence_data`
- **Description**: Compares Genetic Algorithm (teal) and Particle Swarm Optimization (orange) convergence over 175 iterations, with the y-axis showing objective function value.
- **Interpretation**: 
  - Both algorithms reduce the objective value from ~1000 to near 0, stabilizing after ~100 iterations.
  - Genetic Algorithm converges slightly faster initially, while Particle Swarm Optimization shows minor fluctuations. An improvement of ~90-95% indicates effective optimization.

### 2. Energy Flows and Operations
- **Source**: `plot_energy_flows` with `simulation_results` over 168 hours
- **Description**: Four subplots: Renewable Generation and Load Demand (PV, wind, load), Energy Storage Operations (battery, EV), Grid Interactions (purchase, sale), and State of Charge (SOC) with 20-95% constraints.
- **Interpretation**:
  - **Generation and Load**: PV peaks at ~100 kW during daylight, wind averages ~50 kW with variations, and load averages ~120 kW with daily cycles.
  - **Storage Operations**: Battery and EV charge/discharge balance supply and demand, with SOC maintained within constraints.
  - **Grid Interactions**: Purchases occur during deficits, with occasional sales during PV surplus.
  - **SOC**: Battery and EV SOC stay stable, starting at ~50%, ensuring reliable operation.

### 3. Monte Carlo Analysis Results
- **Source**: `plot_monte_carlo_results` with `monte_carlo_results`
- **Description**: Four box plots for No V2G, Managed V2G, and Optimized V2G scenarios: COE ($/kWh), LPSP, REF, and V2G Energy (kWh).
- **Interpretation**:
  - **COE**: Optimized V2G (mean ~0.15, std ~0.02) is more stable and lower than No V2G (~0.20, std ~0.03).
  - **LPSP**: Optimized V2G (median ~0.01, range 0.00-0.02) outperforms No V2G (~0.05, 0.03-0.07) in reliability.
  - **REF**: Optimized V2G (median ~0.8, range 0.75-0.85) shows higher renewable use than No V2G (~0.6, 0.55-0.65).
  - **V2G Energy**: Optimized V2G contributes ~200 kWh (std ~50), highlighting its balancing role.

### 4. Economic Analysis Dashboard
- **Source**: `plot_economic_analysis` with `economic_results`
- **Description**: Seven subplots: Key Metrics (COE 0.145 $/kWh, LPSP 0.005, REF 0.85), Cost Breakdown (72.7% capital, 18.2% operation, 9.1% grid), Reliability vs Cost, Energy Distribution (85% renewable), COE Scenarios (0.181, 0.160, 0.145 $/kWh), NPC ($550,000), and Sustainability Metrics (REF 0.85, independence 0.995, V2G ~0.85).
- **Interpretation**:
  - **Metrics**: Low LPSP and high REF indicate a reliable, renewable system with a competitive COE.
  - **Cost Breakdown**: Capital dominates, offset by low grid costs.
  - **Trade-off**: High reliability (0.995) aligns with low COE.
  - **Energy Distribution**: 85% renewable use reflects sustainability.
  - **COE Comparison**: Optimized V2G reduces COE by ~20% vs. No V2G.
  - **NPC**: $550,000 covers lifecycle costs.
  - **Sustainability**: Strong REF and independence, with V2G matching renewable penetration.

### 5. Optimal Component Sizing Analysis
- **Source**: `plot_component_sizing` with `component_sizing`
- **Description**: Four subplots: Component Counts (500 PV, 10 wind, 12 batteries), Capacity Distribution (PV 162.5 kW, wind 50 kW, battery 424.6 kWh), System Characteristics (212.5 kW renewable, 424.6 kWh storage, 2.5 days autonomy), and Cost Breakdown (PV $100,000, wind $100,000, battery $120,000).
- **Interpretation**:
  - **Counts**: PV dominates with 500 units, supported by 10 wind turbines and 12 batteries.
  - **Capacity**: Battery (66.6%) ensures reliability, with balanced PV (25.5%) and wind (7.8%).
  - **Characteristics**: 2.5 days autonomy and 424.6 kWh storage support off-grid capability.
  - **Cost**: Battery ($120,000) slightly exceeds PV and wind ($100,000 each).

### 6. Parameter Sensitivity Analysis
- **Source**: `plot_sensitivity_analysis` with `sensitivity_results`
- **Description**: Two subplots: Tornado Diagram (PV cost, grid price, load growth impact on COE) and Sensitivity Trends (COE vs. multiplier 0.8-1.2).
- **Interpretation**:
  - **Tornado Diagram**: Grid price (±0.015 $/kWh) has the largest impact, followed by load growth and PV cost (±0.005 $/kWh).
  - **Trends**: COE rises steeply with grid price, moderately with load growth, and slightly with PV cost, confirming grid price sensitivity.

---

## 📝 Conclusion
The plots indicate a well-optimized V2G microgrid with 85% renewable energy use, high reliability (LPSP 0.005), and a COE of 0.145 $/kWh, enhanced by V2G. Battery storage and grid price sensitivity are key factors for cost management and policy focus.
